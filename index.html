About

I am a Professor of Finance at the Smeal College of Business at Penn State. My research is in empirical asset pricing and financial econometrics, including return predictability, the evaluation of asset pricing models, factor models and anomalies, commodity futures, and the performance of mutual funds and hedge funds.

I received my Ph.D. in Finance, with a supporting area in Statistics, from the University of Washington in 2002, and my B.S. in Economics and Finance, summa cum laude, from the University of Texas at Dallas. I served as Director of the Finance Ph.D. program at Penn State from 2015 to 2022 and currently serve as an Associate Editor of the Review of Quantitative Finance and Accounting and the Review of Pacific Basin Financial Markets and Policies.
